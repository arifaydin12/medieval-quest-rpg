Medieval Quest

Forge your destiny in a realm of steel, shadow and stone.

Enter the Realm View the source
🧙

Make your hero

Four races, four classes and four backgrounds, each changing your attributes, gear and abilities.

📜

Live the story

A main story with four endings and a handful of side quests, with choices that move the factions of the realm.

⚔️

Fight turn by turn

Class abilities, status effects, critical hits, bosses and a combat log with floating damage numbers.

⚒️

Trade and craft

Buy and sell with merchants, forge weapons and armour, brew potions and equip rare loot.

🗺️

Explore the wilds

Eight locations, day and night, random events, ambushes and a world map of the kingdom.

💾

Never lose progress

Autosave plus three save slots, all stored in your browser. Works on desktop and phone.

Medieval Quest is open source under the MIT licence. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
